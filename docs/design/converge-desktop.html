Nexus on desktop: three layouts

Today Nexus is a 520px column, centred on any screen. These three layouts take over from 1025px wide, just above the largest common tablet breakpoint (iPad portrait 1024). Below that the current mobile design stays as it is. All three keep Converge and the Ignite snap; poster, title and year appear straight away, only the services are revealed. Click around: search, pick a movie, follow a service. Sample data, not real availability.

Scaled to fit. Use Full screen to see it at your screen's real size. The first time you open a movie you get Discovery; open it again and Nexus has seen it, so you get Ignite. Try searching "matrix", "parasite", "jönsson" or "animatrix" (not in Sweden).
