Converge: three ways to snap

The discovery reveal is unchanged. These are three options for what happens when Nexus already knows the answer, all in Converge’s colors and taking about a quarter of a second. Pick a movie once to see the full discovery, then press Replay the snap or open it again. All data is made up.

Snap 1

Scan

The direct descendant of Signal’s snap. A single beam in the Converge gradient sweeps down the screen, and each row comes into focus as the beam crosses it.

What happens
A cyan-to-magenta line falls from top to bottom in 0.24 seconds. Rows go from slightly blurred to sharp as it passes.
What it says
“Already scanned.” It reads as a quick refresh rather than a search.
Build effort
Lowest. A CSS line and a blur per row, with no canvas.

Snap 2

Ignite

The nexus point lights up and the answer opens out of it, like a screen switching on from a single line of light.

What happens
A bright horizontal line flashes across at the nexus point, then the answer opens upward and downward from it in 0.22 seconds.
What it says
“Straight from the nexus.” It ties the snap to the meeting point of the discovery animation.
Build effort
Low. One clip animation plus a short canvas line.

Snap 3

Rail

The streams are already connected, so one pulse runs down a rail of light through every service and switches each row on.

What happens
A single bright pulse drops from the nexus down the column of service icons. Each icon flares and its row brightens as the pulse passes, all within 0.24 seconds.
What it says
“These connections already exist.” It is the most Converge-specific of the three, since it reuses the stream language.
Build effort
Low to medium. A small canvas trail plus a glow per icon.

Whichever you pick, reduced motion still turns it into a short fade, and a tap skips it.
